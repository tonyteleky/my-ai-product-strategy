## AI Value Archetype
<!-- Automator / Copilot / Oracle / Creator / Orchestrator -->
Oracle (Primary) + Copilot (Secondary)

Oracle: Uses predictive analytics, competitive intelligence, and market data to identify and forecast advertising opportunities.

Copilot: Translates insights into personalized recommendations, helping buyers decide where to invest and sellers decide which advertisers to pursue.



## The Bet in One Sentence
<!-- What you're building, for whom, why now -->
We're building an AI-powered advertising intelligence platform for media buyers and sellers that transforms fragmented market data into predictive insights and actionable recommendations, making it easier to identify and capitalize on advertising opportunities.

## Kill Criteria
<!-- When would you stop? What evidence would kill this bet? -->
We would reconsider or stop the bet if validation with target users reveals that:

Low actionability: Fewer than 30% of users find AI-generated recommendations relevant enough to act on.

Insufficient trust: Users cannot understand or verify the reasoning behind recommendations, preventing adoption.

No measurable improvement: The platform fails to meaningfully reduce research and planning time or improve opportunity identification compared with existing workflows.

Poor data quality: Available advertising data is insufficiently accurate, timely, or comprehensive to generate reliable predictions and matches.
